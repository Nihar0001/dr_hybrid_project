Hybrid Feature Fusion

Our proprietary architecture utilizes a Hybrid Feature Fusion approach. By combining spatial hierarchical features from Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) with global contextual modeling via Vision Transformers (ViT), the system can detect minute retinal micro-aneurysms while maintaining a clinical perspective of the entire eye structure.

  • ● Multi-scale spatial aggregation for early detection.
  • ● Attention-based weighting for lesion localization.
